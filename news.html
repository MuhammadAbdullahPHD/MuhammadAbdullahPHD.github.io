2026 · Publication

Beyond BMI: understanding plantar pressure and gait

Our new article in IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering examines associations between body measurements, plantar pressure and model-predicted joint loading in 21 participants. The exploratory findings support studying detailed anthropometry alongside BMI.

Read the paper

2026 · Publication

Sex-specific pelvis geometry in gait simulations

Our IEEE Access paper investigates how pelvis geometry affects musculoskeletal gait simulations and quantifies bias in male-based models.

Read the paper

2026 · Publication

Body shape and thoracolumbar loading

Our Scientific Reports study explores the influence of body-shape-based mass scaling and thoracic disc stiffness on predicted spinal loading during lateral bending.

ANN-predicted ground reaction forces for joint-load estimation

Our paper in the proceedings of the 47th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society describes a framework for using ANN-predicted ground reaction forces in OpenSim joint-load estimation.
