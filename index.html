About me

Engineering insight for more inclusive movement models

I am a PhD candidate in Engineering, specializing in Mechanical Engineering, at Khalifa University of Science and Technology. My dissertation is titled Gait Biomechanics in the Female Population: An AI-Informed Musculoskeletal Modeling Approach.

Alongside computational modeling, I have supported multimodal data collection at the Khalifa University Gait Laboratory and clinical gait studies involving adults with stroke and children with cerebral palsy. My earlier work spans concentrated solar thermal systems and wind-resource assessment.
